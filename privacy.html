BackDisplay

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 (website visit counting added)

BackDisplay ("the app") is an independent app for the rear screen of the Xiaomi 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max on HyperOS. This page explains what the app does with your information.

In short: almost nothing is collected

BackDisplay has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not sell or share any data. Everything the app knows stays on your phone, except the small install record described below under "Install count and notices".

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings (layout, widgets, wallpapers, apps, reminders, flights, emergency details) are stored only on your device. If you use Android's own backup, Android may include them in your Google backup, which Google handles.
  • Face wake and rear face unlock. The rear camera is used on the device only. Images are processed in memory and never saved or sent anywhere. The face template for rear unlock is stored encrypted on your phone.
  • Microphone. Used only while you tap the voice button. Speech is recognised by your phone's own speech service, under that service's terms. BackDisplay does not record or keep audio.
  • Notifications and calls. With your permission, BackDisplay reads notifications and incoming calls to show them on the rear screen. They never leave the phone.
  • Diagnostic log. A small technical log (at most 1 MB) stays on the phone. It leaves the phone only if you send a problem report yourself.

Internet requests

The app fetches public information only: weather for the city you choose (Open-Meteo), and crypto and stock prices (Binance, Yahoo Finance) if you add those widgets. These requests contain no personal data; like any request, those services can see your IP address.

Install count and notices

So the developer knows how many phones use BackDisplay and can reach them if a version stops working, the app sends, about once a day, a random install ID (made by the app, not linked to you or your phone's IDs), the phone model and maker, the Android version, the system build number and the app version. Nothing else: no name, number, account, location, contacts or app data. It is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which only the developer can read, and deleted on request. The app also reads the developer's notices from there (for example "this version is retired, get the new one"); that read sends nothing about you. If the app can't reach Firebase for 7 days, it asks you to go online once before it carries on. Like any request, Google can see your IP address.

Problem reports

If you tap "Report a problem", your email app opens with a report. It holds the diagnostic log, the phone model and an app-specific device ID. Nothing is sent unless you press Send yourself, and reports are used only to fix the problem.

Shizuku

BackDisplay controls the rear screen through Shizuku, a separate free app you install and start yourself. It works only while Shizuku is running and only as long as Shizuku, Android and HyperOS allow it. A system update can stop the rear-screen features.

This website

The website counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter: it sets no cookies, doesn't track you across sites and stores no personal data. It records the page, the referring site, the browser and screen size, and the country (worked out from your IP address, which isn't stored). It also counts clicks on the download and support buttons, without what you copy or pay. The app itself sends nothing to GoatCounter.

Support payments

BackDisplay is free. If you choose to support it, the payment goes through the service you pick (Binance, your bank, a mobile wallet or a card platform). The developer receives only what that service shows to a recipient, and never your card details.

Children

BackDisplay is not directed at children under 13.
