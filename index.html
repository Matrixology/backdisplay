FAQ

Questions

Which phones does it work on?

Xiaomi 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max, 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max on HyperOS. On phones without a rear screen it does nothing.

Why does it need Shizuku?

Android doesn't let normal apps draw on the rear screen. Shizuku gives BackDisplay that access without root. BackDisplay works only while Shizuku is running, and a system update can change what is allowed.

How do I update?

Come back here and download the new APK. It installs over the old one and keeps your settings.

Is it safe? What data does it collect?

None. No account, no ads, no analytics. Face wake runs on the phone and nothing leaves it. Privacy policy.

Is the app made by Xiaomi?

No. BackDisplay is an independent app and is not affiliated with Xiaomi, Google or Shizuku.
